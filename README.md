## How it works

1. **Describe or upload.** Write what the figure should show, or upload your data file.
2. **Refine.** Edit labels, counts, colors and layout in the online editor. Switch between colorblind-safe palettes (Okabe-Ito, Nature, Science, Cell) in one click.
3. **Export.** PNG for drafts; SVG and PDF on Pro for journal submission.

Free plan: daily credits, watermarked PNG export, no credit card. Pro: editable SVG and PDF. See [pricing](https://figcanvas.com/pricing).

## FAQ

**Is FigCanvas free?** Yes. The free plan gives daily credits and exports watermarked PNGs. Pro adds editable SVG and PDF export.

**Is FigCanvas a BioRender alternative?** For many figures, yes. BioRender is built around placing icons from its library; FigCanvas generates the whole figure from a description or from your data, and puts illustrations, plots and flowcharts on the same canvas.

**Can I use AI-generated figures in a journal?** It depends on the journal. Check the publisher's policy on AI images, disclose AI use where required, and verify every label before you submit.
